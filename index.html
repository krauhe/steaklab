From the manual: maximum power

The rating is electrical input (or fuel input for gas). Transfer is an editable technology-based estimate, not a measurement. Use the normal maximum, excluding Boost. Electric plate types share a rough default; actual pan size and contact matter.

Optional: time to boil 1 litre

Use your frying pan on the same cooking zone, without a lid. Start with 1 litre of water and the pan at the same temperature. Use the highest numbered setting, without Boost, with other zones off. Time from switching on until sustained boiling; then switch off.

20 °C is an assumption for room-temperature water, not cold tap water. Boiling is assumed at 100 °C (near sea level).
